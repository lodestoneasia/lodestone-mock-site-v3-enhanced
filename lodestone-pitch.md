# The Lodestone Centre, Spoken Pitch

*Use this when someone asks "what is The Lodestone?", at a party, in a school hallway, on a podcast, anywhere. Three lengths plus a few utility phrases.*

## The one-liner (8 seconds)

> *"We're a centre that specialises in personalised learning for every child. Some have learning differences, some are gifted, some just don't fit a one-size-fits-all classroom. We build the program around how your child actually learns."*

## The 30-second version (most common)

> *"We're called The Lodestone, like a compass stone. We specialise in personalisation. That means we work with children who have dyslexia, ADHD, autism, gifted kids who need more challenge, and kids who just deserve learning built around them. Every program starts with your child's strengths, not a label. The first 15-minute call is free, no diagnosis required. Parents tell us their child finally feels seen."*

## The 60-second story version

> *"Imagine a parent comes to us. Their child is seven, struggling at school, and the teacher keeps saying 'don't worry, they'll grow out of it.' Maybe it's dyslexia. Maybe it's nothing. Maybe the child is bored. They're not sure.*
>
> *The first thing we do is sit with them for fifteen minutes. Free, no diagnosis required, no sales pitch. We listen. Then if it makes sense, we screen. Then we build a program around what the child can do, not what they can't.*
>
> *Six months later that kid is reading Roald Dahl, and the parent tells us they cry every time they think about how different things feel now. That's what we do. We're called The Lodestone because every child deserves a direction."*

## Three "what makes you different" zingers

- *"Most centres lead with diagnosis. We lead with personalisation. Every program starts with your child, not their label."*
- *"We don't try to fix your child. We help them learn how they learn. There's a difference."*
- *"You don't need a diagnosis to start with us. A lot of our families come in unsure, and we screen first if it helps."*

## The phrases to repeat

- *"Where every child finds their direction."*
- *"We specialise in personalisation. That's it."*
- *"Neurodivergent, gifted, and everyone between."*
- *"We start with what's right, not what's wrong."*
- *"No diagnosis required to begin."*

## The closer (always ready)

> *"If you ever know a family who's stuck, kid bored, kid struggling, school saying wait and see, send them our way. The first call is free, fifteen minutes, no pressure. We're on WhatsApp."*

## The professional variant (for paediatricians, schools, OTs)

> *"We're a Malaysia-based education centre that specialises in personalised learning. Six core programs across speech, SEL, screening, multi-sensory math, coding, and IGCSE crash, plus an early-intervention program for ages 2 to 6 grounded in Japanese-virtue pedagogy. Strengths-first across the board. We work with neurodivergent learners, gifted students, and any child who needs learning built around them. Specialist-led, maximum 1-to-4 ratio. Happy to send referral materials."*
